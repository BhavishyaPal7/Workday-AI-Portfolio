# AI Design Principles

## Purpose

This document defines the enterprise AI design principles followed while designing Workday AI solutions in this portfolio.

---

## Principle 1 – AI Assists, Humans Decide

AI should generate recommendations, summaries, and insights. Final HR decisions must always remain with human managers.

---

## Principle 2 – Use Verified Workday Data

AI should prioritize verified data such as Goals, Competencies, Feedback, Learning History, and Skills over free-text assumptions.

---

## Principle 3 – Detect Conflicts, Don't Resolve Them

When different sources provide conflicting information, the AI should highlight the conflict instead of making assumptions.

---

## Principle 4 – Confidence Before Conclusions

Every AI-generated recommendation should be supported by a confidence score based on available evidence.

---

## Principle 5 – Apply Enterprise Guardrails

AI must follow security, business, and compliance policies before generating any response.

---

## Principle 6 – Human-in-the-Loop

Business-critical actions such as performance ratings, promotions, and compensation decisions must always require human approval.

---

## Key Takeaway

Enterprise AI should support human decision-making, not replace it.
